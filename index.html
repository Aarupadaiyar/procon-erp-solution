01The problem

What running six steel verticals on spreadsheets actually costs.

01

Stock split across 3 plants

  • Pondicherry, Vannur, Chennai each track stock alone
  • No live combined view of steel on hand
  • One plant over-orders while another runs short
02

Material proof goes missing

  • Heat numbers and test certificates filed on paper
  • Railway, RDSO and BHEL audits need them in minutes
  • A missing certificate can hold up a payment
03

RA bills raised by hand

  • Government and PSU jobs bill in stages, not one shot
  • Retention percent tracked in someone's notebook
  • Late RA bill means late payment, every time
04

Six verticals, one generic sheet

  • PEB, bridges, rail cars, boiler jobs cost differently
  • All forced into the same spreadsheet or module
  • Real project margin stays a guess until the job closes
05

Site work is a blind spot

  • Material sent to erection sites, rarely tracked back
  • Sub-contractor labour and wastage go unrecorded
  • Losses only surface at final job costing, too late
06

GST and TDS tracked by hand

  • Works contract GST rules differ from product GST
  • Client TDS deductions need manual matching
  • One missed entry becomes an audit headache
07

Tender to invoice, five files deep

  • Quote, PO, fabrication, dispatch, erection, billing, all separate
  • Nobody sees one project's full status in one place
  • Follow-ups happen by memory, not by system
08

Plant to plant transfer, unrecorded

  • Steel moves between Pondicherry, Vannur and Chennai often
  • Transfers rarely logged the moment they happen
  • Stock count drifts further from reality every month
